# Clínica Clipe — Website concept (approved)

## Decisions
- **Concept:** "Cuidado em todas as fases". Clipe is a pediatric clinic with specialties that grows with the family, from pregnancy to adulthood.
- **Slogan:** "Cuidando do hoje, para um futuro melhor."
- **Visual direction:** premium and warm, matching the new reception and facade (wood, cream, gold, lilac). Not the colourful flyer style.
- **Language:** Portuguese only.
- **Logo:** original line logo, redrawn as a vector in the same style. Lilac letters, brown subtitle. Files are in `logo/`.
- **Main name:** Clipe, Pediatria e Especialidades (from the facade).
- **Vacinas Store:** a sub-brand inside the clinic, with its own page.

## Services by phase
- **Gestação:** prenatal pediatric consultation, nutrition for pregnant women
- **Nascimento:** pediatrician in the delivery room (low and high risk), the pezinho, orelhinha, olhinho, coraçãozinho, linguinha and bochechinha tests, phototherapy, frenotomy, first ear piercing
- **Infância e adolescência:** pediatrics, vaccines (Vacinas Store), psychology, neurofunctional physiotherapy, nutrition, neuropediatrics
- **Toda a família:** general surgery (hernia repair, vasectomy, perineoplasty, hysterectomy, gallbladder removal, minor surgeries), cardiology, spirometry, IV therapy, laser therapy, injectables
